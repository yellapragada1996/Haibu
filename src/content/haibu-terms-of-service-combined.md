## Part 2 — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Haibu collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform, whether as a guest or a creator. Haibu is subject to Canada's federal privacy law (PIPEDA).

### 2.1 What we collect

**Account information:** your email address, display name, and (for creators) a public bio, category, and offering details.

**Payment information:** when you make or receive a payment, our payment processor, Stripe, collects and stores your payment details directly. Haibu does not see or store your full card number.

**Booking information:** session times, the offering booked, and timestamps recording when a session started and ended (used for our cancellation and refund policy).

**Identity verification (creators only):** creators must complete identity verification through Stripe Identity before they can receive payouts. This verification is handled directly by Stripe; Haibu stores only a confirmation that verification was completed, not the underlying documents.

**Communications:** if you contact our support, we keep a record of that conversation to help resolve your issue.

**What we do not collect:** Haibu does not record video or audio from sessions. We do not have a recording feature, and we do not store any content from your live calls.

### 2.2 How we use your information

We use your information to operate your account, process bookings and payments, send transactional emails (confirmations, reminders, verification codes), enforce our Terms, investigate reports of misconduct, and improve the Service. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

### 2.3 Cookies

Haibu currently uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for the Service to function — for example, keeping you logged in and enabling secure payment processing through Stripe. We do not currently use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and no consent banner is required for the essential cookies we use. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and, where legally required, ask for your consent before those cookies are set.

### 2.4 Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their specific function and are contractually bound to handle it securely. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

## Part 3 — Refund Policy

### If you need to cancel a session

| When you cancel | What you get back |
|---|---|
| More than 24 hours before your session | Full refund |
| Within 5 minutes of booking, no matter how soon the session is | Full refund |
| Between 24 hours and 2 hours before your session | 50% refund |
| Less than 2 hours before your session | No refund |
| After your session's scheduled start time | Cancellation is no longer possible at this point |

Refunds are issued automatically to your original payment method through Stripe. Depending on your bank, refunds can take a few business days to appear on your statement.

### If a creator cancels your session

You always receive a **full refund**, no matter when the creator cancels. There is no exception to this.

### If a creator doesn't show up, or leaves early

Every session on Haibu is protected. If a creator misses your session entirely, or leaves significantly before the scheduled end time, you're automatically refunded a fair amount for the time you didn't receive — you don't need to request it.

- If a creator never joins your session, you receive a **full refund**.
- If a creator joins late or leaves early, your refund is calculated based on how much of your booked time was actually delivered.

### If you don't show up to your session

If you don't join your session, no refund applies — the creator has set aside that time for you and is compensated for it.

### How session timing works

Your session runs for the exact length you booked, starting at the scheduled time — it does not extend to make up for either party joining late. We recommend joining a couple of minutes early.

### If something goes wrong that isn't covered above

Contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL — fill in] within 72 hours of your session. We review these requests individually.

## Part 4 — Community Guidelines

Haibu is a place for genuine, friendly, one-on-one connection — casual conversation, ASMR, music, and similar categories. Everyone on Haibu should feel comfortable, respected, and safe.

### Keep it SFW

Every session on Haibu must remain suitable for a general audience. This applies to both guests and creators, in every category. Sexually explicit conduct, nudity, or content of that nature is never allowed on Haibu, in any session type.

### No recording

Recording, screen-capturing, or otherwise saving any part of a session is not allowed without the clear, explicit consent of the other person. Haibu does not provide a built-in recording feature.

### Be who you say you are

Don't impersonate someone else, misrepresent your identity, or pretend to be a different creator or guest.

### No harassment

Harassment, threats, hate speech, or abusive behavior toward another user is not tolerated.

### Stay on the platform

Bookings, payments, and communication related to a session should stay on Haibu. Don't ask another user to move a paid interaction off-platform to avoid Haibu's payment system or protections.

### If something feels wrong

You can report a concern about a specific session or user directly from your booking, or block a user entirely, using the tools built into the app. If you feel unsafe during a live session, you can leave at any time — you don't need permission.

Reports are reviewed by our team. Depending on what happened, outcomes can include a warning, cancellation of future bookings between the two parties, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from Haibu.

### Category-specific notes

- **ASMR and casual talk** sessions should stay warm and personal, but everything above still applies.
- **Music** sessions should be a genuine lesson, performance, or musical interaction as described in the offering.
